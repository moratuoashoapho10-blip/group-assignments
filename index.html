About Lesotho

"The Mountain Kingdom — where every road leads to an adventure."

Lesotho, officially the Kingdom of Lesotho, is a landlocked country nestled entirely within South Africa. Known as the "Kingdom in the Sky," it is the only nation on Earth where all land sits above 1,000 metres above sea level.

From the snow-dusted peaks of the Maluti and Drakensberg mountains to the flowing rivers and vibrant Basotho culture, Lesotho offers a travel experience unlike any other on the continent.

  • Unique highland landscape and breathtaking scenery
  • Rich Basotho culture, traditions, and heritage
  • World-class hiking, skiing, and adventure sports
  • Warm and welcoming local communities
  • Safe destination with authentic African experiences
